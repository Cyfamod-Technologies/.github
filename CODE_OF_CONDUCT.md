# Cyfamod Open-Source Code of Conduct

Cyfamod Technologies is committed to a professional, welcoming and useful open-source environment.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus feedback on the work;
- welcome questions and explain decisions without belittling others;
- respect different experience levels, backgrounds and perspectives;
- give credit for ideas and contributions;
- protect private, customer and security-sensitive information;
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope and project direction.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following is not acceptable in Cyfamod project spaces:

- harassment, threats, discrimination or targeted insults;
- sexualised language or unwanted personal attention;
- publishing another person's private information;
- deliberate disruption, spam or repeated bad-faith participation;
- security abuse, credential exposure or attempts to access systems without authorization;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to **info@cyfamod.com** with the subject **Open-source conduct report**. Include relevant links, dates and context. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Cyfamod maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation or take other proportionate action to protect the community and projects.
